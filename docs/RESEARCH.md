# Background research

## 1. Why people come to hate a racial or ethnic group

These drivers come up again and again in research on prejudice (Allport's
*The Nature of Prejudice*, integrated threat theory, realistic conflict theory,
social identity theory). The classifier's "reason" categories are built on
them.

| # | Driver | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Economic competition** | The group is seen as taking jobs, depressing wages, or straining housing and services. Strongest during downturns and visible migration. |
| 2 | **Cultural / symbolic threat** | Their food, religion, language or customs are framed as a threat to "our way of life". |
| 3 | **Demographic anxiety** | "There are too many of them", or talk of "invasion" and "replacement". It rises fast when one migrant group grows quickly. |
| 4 | **In-group favouritism** | People naturally favour their own group. Out-groups get judged on their worst members. |
| 5 | **Low or skewed contact** | Without real personal contact, people form views from memes, news and anecdotes. |
| 6 | **Scapegoating** | A visible minority gets blamed for broad problems: crime, housing, unemployment. |
| 7 | **Media and meme amplification** | Viral clips and copy-paste jokes turn one incident into "what they are all like". |
| 8 | **Political rhetoric** | Politicians and influencers can normalise hostility for votes or engagement. |
| 9 | **Historical and geopolitical conflict** | Wars, partitions and rivalries spill over onto ordinary people (e.g. India–Pakistan). |
| 10 | **Status and hierarchy** | Colourism, class contempt, or resentment when a group does "too well". |
| 11 | **Disgust-based prejudice** | Hygiene and smell stereotypes. These are especially dehumanising and spread easily. |
| 12 | **Fear of crime and fraud** | Real fraud and crime by some members gets generalised to the whole group. |

## 2. Summary of online sentiment toward Indians (as of 2024–2026)

Indians are now one of the largest and most visible migrant groups online. That
is partly the diaspora's size (≈ 35 million) and partly India's huge internet
population. Several trends show up repeatedly in reporting and research:

- **Sharp rise in anti-Indian content online since 2023.** Researchers and
  journalists tracking X, Reddit and 4chan reported a surge in slurs (notably
  "pajeet") and in meme formats aimed at Indians. These spikes followed debates
  over H-1B visas in the US and student and immigration numbers in Canada, the
  UK and Australia.
- **Immigration and jobs are the dominant frame** in Anglophone countries: the
  US (H-1B, tech layoffs, "Indians hiring Indians"), Canada (international
  students, housing, temporary workers), the UK and Australia (migration
  levels).
- **Disgust and hygiene memes** are the most viral and most dehumanising strand:
  street defecation, "smell", dirty food videos. They are often recycled from a
  handful of clips.
- **Scam stereotypes** are fuelled by real call-centre fraud aimed at Western
  pensioners, then generalised to all Indians.
- **Gendered stereotypes:** Indian men portrayed as sexually aggressive online
  ("bobs and vegana" spam), and Indian women as oppressed.
- **Regional and geopolitical hostility:** India–Pakistan and India–Bangladesh
  tensions, plus resentment in some neighbouring countries over India's
  regional influence.
- **Gulf states:** a different pattern, mostly class contempt toward low-wage
  workers rather than "replacement" rhetoric.
- **Counter-currents:** much of the online conversation is positive (tech
  leaders, food, cricket, Bollywood). Indians also tend to score as a
  high-earning, educated diaspora in the US, UK and Canada. A lot of the
  hostility is concentrated in a fairly small number of very active accounts,
  which is why this index averages **per author**.

## 3. Common stereotypes about Indians (and why they're wrong)

The classifier tags each of these. They are listed so they can be recognised
and measured, not because they are true.

| Stereotype | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Indians smell / smell of curry" | Smell-based slurs have been used against nearly every migrant group (Italians, Irish, Chinese). Spice aromas from cooking aren't body odour. |
| "Indians are dirty / defecate in the street" | Rural open defecation was a sanitation-infrastructure problem. It fell sharply after 2014 (Swachh Bharat). It says nothing about migrants abroad. |
| "Indians are scammers" | Organised call-centre fraud exists and is prosecuted in India too. Its victims include Indians. It is the work of a tiny criminal fraction of 1.4 billion people. |
| "Indians take our jobs / abuse H-1B" | Visa programmes are designed and capped by the host government. Indians on them are legally hired, mostly in shortage fields. |
| "Indians only hire other Indians" | Network hiring happens in every community. Where real discrimination occurs, it's individual misconduct that labour law covers, not a group trait. |
| "Indian men are creeps / harass women" | Driven by viral spam and a few cases. Harassment exists in every society. |
| "Indians are cheap / stingy" | A frugality stereotype that also gets applied to Scots, Jews and Chinese people. Often it's just high savings rates. |
| "Indians are all tech support / IT workers" | Reductive. The diaspora works across medicine, law, business, trades and the arts. |
| "Indians are poor / third world" | India is the world's 4th–5th largest economy. The diaspora is among the highest-earning groups in several countries. |
| "Indians worship cows / are backward / superstitious" | Mockery of religious practice. Every religion has rituals that look strange from outside. |
| "Indians are loud / rude / no queueing" | Behaviour norms differ by culture and individual. The stereotype is applied to many groups. |
| "Indian food makes you sick ('Delhi belly')" | Traveller's diarrhoea happens anywhere with unfamiliar microbes. Indian food is eaten safely worldwide. |
| "Indians are bad drivers" | A generic stereotype applied to many immigrant groups, with no group-level evidence. |
| "Indians are overrunning / invading" | Replacement rhetoric. Indians are a single-digit share of the population in every Western country. |
| "Indian degrees/doctors are fake" | Credential fraud exists everywhere. Indian-trained doctors and engineers pass the same host-country licensing exams. |
